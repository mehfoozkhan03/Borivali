Visualizing ch & ex Units

This demo renders both units at a large font size (24px) to clearly map out how browsers derive layout values directly from the structural anatomy of a font.

Proportional Font (Georgia)
CH Unit Grid (10ch wide container): Width = 10 zeros
0000000000
Notice how variable letters like "iiiiiiiiii" or "WWWWWWWWWW" won't align perfectly to this grid.
EX Unit Track: Height = Lowercase x AaBbCxyZ
