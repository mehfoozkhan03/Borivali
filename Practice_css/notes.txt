-  clasification of css unit

# Absolute Units:- 
    Absolute unit represent fixed measurement. They don't depend on the font size of the parent elements or the dimentions of a containing elements.

    A. Pixels(PX)
    B. Centimeter(CM)
    C. Millemeter(MM)
    D. Inches(IN)
    E. Points(PT)
    F. Picas(PC)
